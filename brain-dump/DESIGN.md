## Brand & Style
The brand personality is elegant, highly structured, and timeless. It draws inspiration from high-end editorial layouts and premium productivity tools (like Notion or Linear). It uses stark black and white contrast, delicate borders, and highly structured grid systems.

## Colors
The palette is strictly monochromatic. Pure white backgrounds with pure black text for maximum contrast. Subdued grays (`#e5e5e5`, `#f5f5f5`) are used for subtle container backgrounds and very thin 1px borders to divide content. 

## Typography
The system uses **Inter** throughout. Large display fonts are heavy and tightly tracked to feel premium. Body fonts are clean and highly legible.

## Layout & Components
- **Borders:** Ultra-thin 1px borders (#e5e5e5) to define structure instead of shadows or gradients.
- **Buttons:** Sharp or slightly rounded corners (4px). Primary buttons are pure black.
- **Space:** Heavy reliance on whitespace and negative space.
